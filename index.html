About

Learning, uncertainty, and financial decision-making

I am an undergraduate student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University studying Mathematics and Applied Mathematics with a double degree in Artificial Intelligence. My work explores how probabilistic machine learning and uncertainty-aware models can support robust decisions in financial markets.

I am particularly interested in models that connect representation learning, uncertainty quantification, and portfolio construction while remaining transparent and reproducible.

Selected research

Independent research project

VESTA

Variational Experts with Soft Routing for Tail-Aware Portfolio Management

A research project studying uncertainty-aware expert routing, heavy-tailed return modeling, and portfolio allocation for cross-sectional equity selection. The project combines temporal representation learning with probabilistic routing and cost-aware portfolio objectives.
